Surgical Preparedness Index

A validated tool to help hospitals assess their resilience and preparedness for planned surgical services during periods of external system stress.

Published in The Lancet 2022; 400:1607–17
📊
About the SPI

The SPI was developed by the NIHR Global Health Research Unit on Global Surgery (now the Surgical Data and Policy Lab) at the University of Birmingham. It provides a standardised, quantitative measure of a hospital's capacity to maintain planned (elective) surgical services when the health system faces external shocks — such as pandemics, conflict, natural disasters, or resource shortfalls.

The index comprises 23 indicators across 4 domains, each scored from 1 (Very Weak) to 5 (Very Strong), yielding a total score of 23–115.

How to Complete This Assessment
  • 1
    Enter Hospital Details

    Provide basic information about your hospital — name, country, income setting, and your professional role.

  • 2
    Rate 23 Indicators

    Score each indicator from 1 (Very Weak) to 5 (Very Strong), reflecting your hospital's current ability to maintain planned surgery during a period of external system stress.

  • 3
    Review Your Results

    See your total SPI score, a breakdown by domain, comparison to global benchmarks, and a prioritised list of areas for improvement.

  • 4
    Export Your Report

    Download a PDF report to share with colleagues or use for local audit and quality improvement.

Note: For optimal accuracy, complete the assessment with multidisciplinary input from surgery, anaesthesia, nursing, and hospital administration teams. This tool is intended for surgical leads, hospital managers, and health system planners.
